🌟 Our Guiding Lights

Six principles to guide us online and beyond

💎

Honesty & Originality

Create with your own hands. Credit where it’s due. True success is earned, not copied.

"Integrity is choosing truth over likes."

💛

Kindness in Every Word

Behind every screen is a heart. Speak gently, reply patiently, lift others up.

"Be someone’s favorite part of the internet."

🔍

Truth Before Sharing

Stop. Check. Verify. Don’t spread what you haven’t confirmed. Truth lasts longer than trends.

"Think before you click. Verify before you share."

🤝

Respect for Everyone

Differences make us beautiful. Listen first, speak second. Honor dignity always.

"Respect is free — give it generously."

🛡️

Protect & Uplift

Use technology to help, not harm. Build spaces where everyone feels safe and welcome.

"Make the web brighter than you found it."

🌅

Faith in Action

Let your values guide your choices. Be a light that cannot be hidden — online and everywhere.

"Your digital footprint is your witness."
